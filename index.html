FAQs

Good questions. Clear answers.

How do I know if my terrace needs waterproofing?

Common signs include damp ceilings below the terrace, water stains, pooling, cracked surfaces and recurring leakage after rainfall. A site inspection can help identify the cause.

Can waterproofing be done on an existing building?

Yes, various systems can be applied to existing structures. The method depends on the condition of the substrate and where water is entering.

How long does waterproofing take?

Timelines vary based on the area, surface preparation, chosen system and curing requirements. Ask the team for a project-specific timeline.

How much will waterproofing cost?

Costs depend on area, damage, access, preparation and material system. Send your approximate area and problem details to request a tailored discussion.
